Beginner Roadmap

Build. Improve. Pitch.

Study the fundamentals.

Create 3–5 portfolio samples.

Contact potential clients.

The Takeaway

AI isn't your freelance business.

Your skill is. AI is the tool that can help you work faster, research better, and improve your workflow.
